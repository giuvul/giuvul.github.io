I’m Giuseppe, Chief Data Officer at E23. I came to data from mathematics, and I still approach every system the same way: find the structure first, then build.

At E23 I designed and directed the platform that profiles airport passengers for retail, from the pipelines to the dashboards our clients use every day. Today I’m extending that work into AI engineering, with agents that run part of the company’s operations.
